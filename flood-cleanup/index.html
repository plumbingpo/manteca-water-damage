Floods start in many ways: heavy rain, storm surge, a river over its banks, a dead sump pump, a sewer backup or a major plumbing failure, and all of them call for fast professional help. Expect gray water (Category 2) or black water (Category 3) in almost any flood, and with it bacteria, contamination and potentially hazardous substances that threaten your health. Day or night, 24/7, Manteca Restoration sends flood cleanup crews throughout Manteca to remove floodwater, sanitize the affected rooms and safely restore your home or building.

We start with fast water extraction, using truck-mounted and submersible pumps that can move thousands of gallons per hour. With the standing water gone, our IICRC-certified technicians check the contamination level and apply the right sanitizing steps, including antimicrobial treatment, HEPA air filtration and disinfection of every affected surface. Anything unsalvageable comes out and is hauled off correctly, including wet insulation, drywall that took in contaminated water and waterlogged carpet padding.

Then the drying begins: high-velocity air movers and commercial dehumidifiers of the LGR (low-grain refrigerant) type go in to pull moisture out of the building's structural parts. Our crew returns daily, using thermal imaging and calibrated meters to track moisture until the structure is completely dry and mold growth is prevented. Every stage of the job is recorded for your claim: photographs, detailed moisture maps, logs of the equipment used and the scope-of-work reports that insurance adjusters require.

Maintenance Tips for Flood Cleanup

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Extend your downspouts to carry rain from the roof out past the foundation
  • Wherever flooding is likely, get appliances and electrical equipment up above floor level
  • Make sure the ground slopes away from your foundation on every side
  • Clear leaves and debris from storm drains and gutters, which lets water drain the way it should
  • Have a backwater valve fitted to the sewer line so sewage cannot flow back inside
  • Store valuables and important papers off the basement floor in waterproof containers

Why Hire a Professional for Flood Cleanup?

There is much more to a flood cleanup than removing the water you can see. The water brings sewage, chemicals, bacteria and more with it, and all of that is a serious hazard to health. A trained cleanup team knows how to set containment, wear PPE (personal protective equipment) correctly and sanitize by protocol, which protects your household from hepatitis, E. coli and other diseases carried in water.

Health is not the only issue: when cleanup is done wrong, moisture stays hidden and heavy mold can follow in a matter of days. With thermal imaging, psychrometric calculations and moisture meters, trained technicians confirm that subfloors, structural framing, wall cavities and every other wet area dry to an acceptable standard. Drying by measurement is the one dependable method for avoiding secondary damage, which can add thousands to the repair bill.

Warning Signs for Flood Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Basement flooding after the sump pump quit in a downpour
  • Water coming in through foundation cracks, window wells or door thresholds
  • Ground so saturated that puddles sit against the foundation walls
  • Appliances such as a water heater, washer or dishwasher spilling water across the floor
  • Living areas flooded by contaminated water from a backed-up drain or sewer
  • Standing water anywhere on your property, whatever the source
  • Carpet that squishes underfoot or drywall that feels soft and wet
