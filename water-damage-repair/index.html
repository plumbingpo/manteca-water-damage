How to Tell You Need Water Damage Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • During drying, sections of wallboard came out and the framing is still exposed.
  • Since the water damage, certain doors drag on the jamb or miss the latch.
  • After drying, the hardwood is still cupped, crowned or lifting.
  • Walking over the formerly wet area, you feel soft spots in the floor.
  • A leak overhead left the ceiling drywall cracked, sagging or falling apart.
  • Grout is breaking up and some tiles have lifted where moisture reached the floor below.
  • Seams are opening, or edges are curling and swelling, on vinyl plank or laminate.

Water Damage Repair Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Future repairs match better when you have held on to extra flooring, tile and the paint information.
  • Fix the source first, and only then patch or paint a water stain.
  • Store pictures of the finished work alongside the scope for future reference.
  • Laundry rooms, bathrooms and kitchens should be rebuilt with moisture resistant wallboard and proper backer board.
  • Curing time for paint, floor adhesive and caulk should pass before the room sees water or hard use.
  • In case the insurance company asks down the road, photograph anything damaged while it is still in place.

Water Damage Repair, Step by Step

Our systematic approach delivers quality results on every project. Here's how we handle water damage repair from start to finish:

1

Confirm It Is Dry

Our crew meters the remaining drywall, the subfloor and the framing one last time. Reconstruction only begins once those numbers are at normal levels for the material, so nothing damp gets sealed in.

2

Write the Scope

We hand you a list, room by room, covering what gets replaced, planned materials and the likely schedule. On jobs with an active claim, our team reconciles that scope with the adjuster.

3

Rebuild the Structure

Open wall cavities get new insulation, the subfloor is fixed, and drywall goes up, then is taped and finished. After that we install trim, cabinetry and floors, ordering the work so completed surfaces are not damaged.

4

Finish and Walk Through

The last stage covers texture matching, primer, paint and putting hardware, doors and baseboards back. Before calling it done, our team tours all the rooms with you and takes care of each item noted.
